Chapter I · The First Age

The Beginning

An age of new beginnings has dawned upon the world.

World · Aethermoor Start · Sanctum of Dawn Sovereign · Normal

Long after the creation of the heavens, earth, and all living things by Elarris, The Goddess, civilizations have flourished across the lands. What once began as small settlements has gradually developed into villages, castles, and powerful kingdoms.

Under the watchful presence of Elarris, the world enjoyed an age of growth and prosperity. Yet as civilizations expanded, so did their ambitions. Different rulers began establishing their own domains, each seeking to secure their people's future and strengthen their influence.

At the heart of the world lies the Divine Shrine, surrounded by the mysterious Sacred Lands. Few dare to approach these sacred territories, where ancient creatures continue to dwell.

Now, a new era is beginning. Kingdoms stand ready to shape the fate of the world, and the ambitions of their rulers will determine what comes next.

Will one kingdom rise above all others and unite the world under a single banner?

Or will the world remain divided among competing powers?

The story of a new age begins here.

Prologue · The Last Dawn

Before the stars
went out

Long ages ago, Elarris, the Goddess of Dawn, sealed the Hollow King beneath the Shadow Citadel. Her light became the sun. Her vow became the border between the living world and the dark.

Now the seal is broken. The sun has dimmed to a dying ember, and the citadel's legions march under a banner of rot. You are Lux Osborn, the young king of the Imperial Kingdom — raised to prove his worth, and now called to prove it once more. One host, one sanctum, one promise stand between the realm and the dark.

“Hold the light. Break the Hollow King. Unite the realm.”

  • ✦Hold the Sanctum of Dawn. If it falls, the light is lost.
  • ✦March across Aethermoor. Take the Shadow Citadel to win.
  • ✦Spend faith wisely. Muster your host, or call down divine powers.
